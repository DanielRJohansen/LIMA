Setup

NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090 (driver 595.84), 11th Gen Intel(R) Core(TM) i5-11600KF @ 3.90GHz, Linux 7.0.11-76070011-generic. lima 1.5.0. GROMACS 2026.4 with CUDA, everything on the GPU (-nb -pme -bonded -update gpu), 6 OpenMP threads.

Median of 2 runs per engine, run alternately. Throughput from the steady state: GROMACS -resethway, LIMA a long minus a short run.

Same physics

2 fs steps, h-bond constraints, PME with each system's original cutoff (0.9-1.2 nm) and grid spacing, v-rescale thermostat. LIMA integrates with velocity Verlet, GROMACS with leap-frog, its fastest path. GROMACS keeps the cutoff fixed (-notunepme).

Systems: Páll et al., J. Chem. Phys. 153, 134110 (2020), force fields as published. ADH, aquaporin and GluCl run as 2 copies, the exact rectangular cell of their triclinic box.

Parity

Forces: the relative RMS difference of every atom's force, LIMA vs GROMACS, for the same structure and settings. 1×10−4 means a typical force differs by 0.01%, ten times less than PME's own approximation of the exact Ewald sum (~10−3).

RMSD: how far the protein's Cα atoms drift from the start, the mean over the second half of a 1 ns run, next to two GROMACS runs. RMSF r: how well LIMA and GROMACS agree on which residues move most (Pearson correlation of per-Cα fluctuations, 1 is identical), next to the agreement between the two GROMACS runs, which differ only in their thermostat seed.

† RMSD and RMSF r from the reference runs (LIMA 88249f24, GROMACS 2024.2, NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4090), not measured on this machine. lima gromacscompare --structure measures them.
