    wrapper still includes (texture_fetch_functions.h, crt/device_runtime.h, device_functions.hpp, math_functions.hpp,
    math_functions_dbl_ptx3.hpp). CUB is under include/cccl since CUDA 13.
  - -DNOMINMAX against windows.h min/max.
Caveats:
  - 87 files had GPU references; the other 20 are host-only LIMA_BASE .cpp files.
  - 37 of the 87 (marked * below) had parse errors, mostly headers examined standalone (missing includes) and STL noise
    under -x cuda, so their counts may be low. The grep inventory and the compile probes below cover what hipify misses.
  - UNCONVERTED refs: 0 in every file. hipify knows every CUDA name it saw; it does not judge warp-mask widths, PTX or
    cooperative_groups features, hence the probes.
